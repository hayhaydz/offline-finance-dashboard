## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Recovery passphrase is CSPRNG-generated diceware material
The recovery passphrase SHALL be generated by the system's CSPRNG from the EFF large wordlist at 12 words (≈128 bits), SHALL be displayed exactly once at enrollment, and SHALL NOT be user-invented or editable. The passphrase wraps a standard recovery slot whose kind is recorded for rotation policy.

#### Scenario: Generated, not chosen
- **WHEN** the user reaches recovery enrollment
- **THEN** the system presents a generated 12-word passphrase with instructions to store it offline, and provides no field to substitute a custom passphrase

#### Scenario: Shown once
- **WHEN** recovery enrollment completes
- **THEN** the passphrase is never displayed again by any surface

### Requirement: Wallet semantics are stated before enrollment is accepted
Recovery enrollment SHALL state, before acceptance, that (a) a forgotten password with a lost passphrase means permanent data loss, (b) the passphrase alone unlocks the data, and (c) passphrase compromise requires full DataKey rotation. Enrollment SHALL be required during account creation and during migration of existing accounts.

#### Scenario: Informed acceptance
- **WHEN** the enrollment screen renders
- **THEN** the three consequences are visible alongside the passphrase and the user must explicitly acknowledge them

#### Scenario: Migration cannot skip recovery enrollment
- **WHEN** an existing account completes the one-shot migration to the envelope
- **THEN** recovery enrollment is completed in the same session before the migration is finalized

### Requirement: Recovery unlock forces re-enrollment
A successful unlock via the recovery passphrase SHALL immediately require enrolling a fresh password slot (and device slot if the user had one) before the app becomes generally usable; the recovery slot SHALL remain valid but the session SHALL be flagged as recovery-initiated for rotation prompts.

#### Scenario: Recovery flow
- **WHEN** the user unlocks with the paper passphrase
- **THEN** they are routed to a re-enrollment flow that must set a new password (and re-enroll a device) before any data-bearing route is usable

### Requirement: Spare credentials are enrollable as primary fallbacks
The system SHALL allow enrolling multiple device slots (e.g. a second authenticator) so that loss of the primary device remains a 2-of-2 unlock via the spare. The settings surface SHALL prompt for spare enrollment when only one device slot exists.

#### Scenario: Spare enrollment
- **WHEN** the user enrolls a second WebAuthn authenticator
- **THEN** either device slot plus the password unlocks, and losing one device does not require the paper passphrase

#### Scenario: Prompt appears with single device
- **WHEN** the security settings render with exactly one enrolled device slot
- **THEN** the UI prompts to enroll a spare

### Requirement: Loss procedures map to the rotation tiers
Losing a device (lost, stolen, or replaced) SHALL be handled by slot revocation and optional re-enrollment with no data re-encryption. Compromise of the recovery passphrase SHALL be handled by full DataKey rotation (all data re-encrypted, all slots re-wrapped). A stolen device's slot SHALL be revoked on report, with the UI noting that a stolen slot alone cannot unlock (2-of-2).

#### Scenario: Stolen phone
- **WHEN** the user reports a stolen enrolled phone and revokes its slot
- **THEN** the slot can no longer be used, the UI states the phone alone could not have unlocked the data, and no data is re-encrypted

#### Scenario: Suspected paper compromise
- **WHEN** the user invokes recovery-compromise rotation
- **THEN** all data is re-encrypted under a fresh DataKey, all slots are re-wrapped, and a new recovery passphrase is generated and shown once

### Requirement: No server-side secret reset exists
No administrative or automated mechanism SHALL reset, re-wrap, or recover the password slot, device slots, or DataKey without the user presenting a valid credential. There SHALL be no backdoor, escrow, or override path.

#### Scenario: No reset endpoint
- **WHEN** the codebase and routes are inspected
- **THEN** no endpoint or script can modify credential slots without an authenticated, unlocked ceremony
